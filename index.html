File 02 · Outcomes & attainment

School Completion Rates

About this data

This shows what share of students actually finish primary, lower secondary, and upper secondary school, at national and state level. Completion rates matter more than enrollment alone, since they capture where students leave the system rather than just how many started. Rates can occasionally exceed 100% due to grade repetition or students transferring between states.

Source: Ministry of Education Malaysia (EMIS), via OpenDOSM · Browse or download the raw dataset

File 03 · Teaching resources

Teachers & Student-Teacher Ratio

About this data

This shows how many teachers work in government primary and secondary schools, by state and nationally, and the resulting student-teacher ratio — calculated by dividing enrollment (File 01) by teacher counts. A lower ratio generally means more individual attention per student.

Source: Ministry of Education Malaysia (EMIS), via OpenDOSM · Browse or download the raw dataset
